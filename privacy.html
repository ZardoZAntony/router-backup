Privacy policy

router-backup is a personal tool used only by its owner.

  • It requests access only to files it creates in the owner's Google Drive (scope drive.file).
  • Backed-up files are sent directly from the owner's router to the owner's Google Drive. They are not sent to any other party.
  • No personal data is collected, sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • The access token is stored only on the owner's router and can be revoked at any time in the Google Account settings.

Questions can be raised through the issue tracker of this repository on GitHub.
